**not** allowed to say.

of the £45 positioning, not a licence to rewrite the site around comparisons.

> **Internal document.** Nothing here is published. No comparative claim drawn

## 4. What the evidence actually supports

**Supported, internally:** £45 for three appliances is below every Wolverhampton
CP12 price found published on 24 August 2026. The closest comparator charging
per appliance (Same Day Certificates) is £69 for the same three appliances; the
closest named local firm (NK Gas) is £80 before any additional appliance.

**Not supported:** any absolute claim about the West Midlands. Eleven named
firms and four aggregators is a sample, not a market. Prices change, "from"
prices hide appliance counts, and nothing here surveys Dudley, Walsall,
West Bromwich, Cannock or Stourbridge — all inside the service radius.

### Claims considered and rejected

Every one of these was considered and **rejected as unsupportable**:

- "Cheapest CP12 in the West Midlands"
- "West Midlands' cheapest gas safety certificate"
- "The lowest price in Wolverhampton"
- "Lowest price guaranteed"
- "Best value in the West Midlands"
- "Cheaper than every other Wolverhampton engineer"
- "Unlike other engineers, we don't charge a call-out fee" — two of the three
  competitors with published terms also state no call-out fee, so this would
  create a false impression
- Any named-competitor price comparison on a marketing page

### What the site says instead

Facts about BSCJ, which need no market survey to defend:

- **Fixed £45 total**, with the appliance coverage stated alongside it
- **Up to three appliances included** (boiler + 2)
- **No separate CP12 call-out charge** — stated as a fact about BSCJ, never as
  a contrast with others
- **Nothing to pay upfront, pay after completion**
- **Book from live availability**, no account, no deposit

## 5. Where BSCJ genuinely differs

Worth knowing internally, and the reason the positioning is "better system and
better value" rather than "cheap":

- **Live online booking.** Every competitor found books by phone or WhatsApp and
  quotes back. BSCJ shows the engineer's real diary and confirms instantly.
- **Price known before contact.** No "send us your address for a quote".
- **All-inclusive appliance count at the base price**, rather than per-appliance
  pricing from one appliance.
- **No cancellation or failed-access charge**, published.

## 6. Review cadence

Re-check before any pricing change, and at least annually. If a comparative
claim is ever wanted, it needs a documented, dated, repeatable survey with a
defined geography — not this file.
